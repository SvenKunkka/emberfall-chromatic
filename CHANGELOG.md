### Added
- Initial native Game Boy Color release for ModRetro Chromatic.
- Ten combat stages, two Wardens, automatic shooting, dash and pulse.
- XP progression, three salvage upgrades, pause, retry and an ending.
- Editable GB Studio project, original pixel-art sources and balance documents.
- Chinese and English project introductions with explicit emulator and physical-hardware validation status.

### Fixed during development
- Sprite position scaling, palette separation, menu clipping and stale HUD text.
- Projectile sprite allocation and per-scanline rendering budget.
